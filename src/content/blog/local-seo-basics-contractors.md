# Local SEO Basics for Small and Medium Businesses

If you run a small or medium business, local SEO is one of the highest-leverage marketing activities you can invest in. The goal is straightforward: show up when local customers search for your services on Google.

Here is a practical overview of what local SEO involves and what actually matters for small and medium businesses.

## What Is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the process of improving your business's visibility in location-based searches. When someone in Vancouver WA searches "[your service] near me" or "[your service] Clark County," Google shows a mix of organic results and a map pack — the three-business box that appears near the top of results.

Appearing in that map pack and in organic results for local searches is the goal of local SEO.

## Google Business Profile Is Essential

Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the most important factor for local map pack visibility. A complete, accurate GBP listing helps you show up when people search for your services in your area.

Key things to do with your GBP:

- Complete every field, including business hours, service area, and service categories
- Add photos of your work, your team, and your location
- Respond to reviews consistently
- Keep your phone number, address, and website URL consistent with what appears on your website

## Service Area Pages

If you serve multiple cities or neighborhoods, you need dedicated pages for each major service area. Without them, Google does not know you serve those areas.

A good service area page includes:

- The city and service type in the title and headings (e.g., "[Your Service] in Camas WA")
- Content that is genuinely useful to a customer in that area
- Your phone number and a contact form
- A mention of other cities you serve nearby

## On-Page SEO Basics

Your website pages need basic on-page optimization to rank for local searches:

- **Title tags** should include your service and location (e.g., "[Your Service] Vancouver WA | Your Business Name")
- **H1 headings** should clearly state what the page is about
- **Meta descriptions** should summarize the page in a way that makes people want to click
- **NAP consistency** — your name, address, and phone number should be identical everywhere online

## Content That Answers Real Questions

One of the most effective things a local service business can do is create content that answers questions their customers actually have. Common examples:

- "How much does [service] cost in [city]?"
- "What is the difference between [option A] and [option B]?"
- "How long does [project type] take?"

These questions are what people search for before deciding who to hire. Answering them on your website builds trust and attracts qualified traffic.

## What Takes Time

Local SEO is not an overnight fix. It typically takes 3–6 months of consistent work before you see meaningful ranking improvements, and longer in competitive markets. This is why it is worth starting early and building consistently.

The fundamentals — a complete GBP, proper on-page SEO, service area pages, and consistent content — compound over time and keep generating results even when you are not actively spending.
